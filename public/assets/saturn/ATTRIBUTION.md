BEYOND uses a closed matte sphere without a solid surface below the clouds.
The body marker follows a representative band at image pixel (700,350), measured
from top-left. Its thin cyan selection outline is explanatory; the information
describes Saturn as a whole, not a named cloud feature or a measured location.

a plain tan sphere keeps travel, rings, and information usable. Creator/license
