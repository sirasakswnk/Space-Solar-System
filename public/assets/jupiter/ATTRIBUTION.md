The Commons copy identifies Solar System Scope and CC BY 4.0. Its description
notes NASA imagery, adjusted saturation, and fictional gap fills. This is a
static illustration of the atmosphere, not its current appearance. The JPEG
is unmodified; BEYOND uses artistic lighting, scale, and a warm neutral tint.

The image is rendered on a closed matte sphere, without a solid surface below.
Cloud bands (720,430) and Great Red Spot (752,627) are image coordinates measured
from top-left, not live atmospheric coordinates. Thin cyan ellipses indicate
selection; their bounds are explanatory, not measured storm boundaries. They
do not cover the image with a fake storm disc or cloud patch. A plain tan sphere
keeps travel and information usable if the texture fails. Creator/license links
appear in the travel caption and Jupiter exploration panel.
